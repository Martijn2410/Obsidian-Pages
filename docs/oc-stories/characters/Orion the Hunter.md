## Bio
Former royalty and the highest-ranking member of The Imagined Order’s Elite Guard. Just before he was crowned the rightful ruler of his kingdom, The IO invaded and conquered his reality in their quest to build the perfect civilization. Feeling robbed of his birthright, he vowed to take back what was his by any means possible.

During his tactical ascent, his ego took another hit when he witnessed a power far greater than his own. The realization that someone out there is stronger leaves him bitterly unsatisfied. He now vows to absorb this ultimate power at any cost. If he can't have it to create and rule his perfect world, nobody can.
