## Bio
A young girl from Gaia, chosen to be the Zero Point’s Chosen One. After a difficult childhood, she vowed to protect the omni-verse from harm, determined that no one else should have to endure the suffering she once faced. With a direct connection to the Zero Point, she possesses an infinite well of energy that never runs dry, fueling her space and time abilities to do good. When paired with her Breaking Point ability, which amplifies her strength fiftyfold, defeating her becomes nearly impossible. She's essentially the strongest mortal out there.
